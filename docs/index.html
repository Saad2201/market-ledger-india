Statutory Revenue Calculation Methodology

This platform applies deterministic Indian statutory tax rules to official primary exchange records. Every calculation is transparent, auditable, and traceable.

1. Central Government: Securities Transaction Tax (STT)

• Equity Delivery: 0.1% buy + 0.1% sell = 0.20% round-trip on delivery value.
• Equity Intraday: 0.025% on sale side of squared-off turnover.
• Futures: 0.02% on sale-side turnover (Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 effective 1 Oct 2024).
• Options Premium: 0.10% on traded sale-side premium (Finance (No. 2) Act 2024).
• STT on Notional Turnover: 0.00% (STT is strictly NOT levied on notional turnover).
• Corporate & Government Debt: 0.00% (Exempt under Finance Act 2008).

2. State Government: Stamp Duty (Indian Stamp Act 1899)

• Levied exclusively on BUY side (Finance Act 2019 / Schedule I Article 27):
• Equity Delivery Buy: 0.015% (₹1,500 / Cr)
• Equity Intraday Buy: 0.003% (₹300 / Cr)
• Futures Buy: 0.002% (₹200 / Cr)
• Options Premium Buy: 0.003% (₹300 / Cr on premium)
• Corporate Debt Buy: 0.0001% (₹10 / Cr)
• G-Sec & SGB: Exempt (Section 8A)

3. Goods and Services Tax (GST: 18%)

• Rule 8.3 & Rule 9: GST is strictly 18% on taxable financial services.
• Taxable Services: Exchange Transaction Charges + SEBI Turnover Fees.
• Turnover Exemption: Traded market turnover is completely exempt from GST.
• Known GST = 18% × (Exchange Fees + SEBI Fees).

4. Delivery Bounds (Floor vs Ceiling)

• Client-level intraday vs delivery segregation is private to clearing corporations.
• Floor: If 100% intraday = Cash Equity Turnover × 0.025%
• Ceiling: If 100% delivery = Cash Equity Turnover × 0.20%
• Modeled: Weighted by official Gross Delivery value from exchange delivery files.

5. Non-Government Intermediary Charges (Segregated)

• Exchange transaction charges (NSE ~₹297/Cr cash, BSE ~₹300/Cr cash) are retained by exchanges as private operating income.
• They are tracked as market friction costs and never added into government revenue totals.

2. Critical Mathematical Distinctions

Options Premium vs. Notional Turnover:
Under the Finance Act, Securities Transaction Tax (STT) and Stamp Duty on options are levied strictly on the Option Premium value traded (or intrinsic value upon exercise), never on the notional contract value.
From NSE FO UDiFF bhavcopy: Option Premium Turnover = Total Transfer Value - (Strike Price × Traded Volume × Lot Size)
Stamp Duty Jurisdiction:
Under the Indian Stamp Act 1899 (Article 27, Schedule I, amended effective 1 July 2020), Stamp Duty on stock market transactions is levied strictly on the BUYER (0.015% delivery, 0.003% intraday, 0.002% futures, 0.003% options premium). Collected by exchanges and remitted to the buyer's State Government.
GST on Financial Services:
Under GST Law, the traded securities value is exempt from GST. GST (18%) is levied strictly on intermediary taxable fees: Exchange Transaction Charges + SEBI Regulatory Turnover Fees.

Statutory Data Coverage & Methodology

MarketLedger — India calculates statutory revenues based on official BhavCopy and settlement data published daily by the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and BSE India.

Tax Classification: • Central Government: STT (Securities Transaction Tax)
• State Governments: Indian Stamp Duty (Buy-side only)
• SEBI: Regulatory Turnover Fee (₹10 per crore)
• GST Pool: 18% on exchange transaction charges and SEBI fees
